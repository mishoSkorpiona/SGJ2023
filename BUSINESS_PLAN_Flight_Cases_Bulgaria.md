# Flight Case Manufacturing Business Plan
## Bulgaria-Based Production Hub

---

## Executive Summary

**Business:** Custom flight case manufacturing for AV, professional audio, touring, and industrial equipment protection.

**Location:** Bulgaria (EU member, low production costs, strategic positioning)

**Target Markets:** 
- Primary: Germany, Netherlands, UK, Scandinavia
- Secondary: Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia), EU-wide
- Tertiary: OEM/white-label partnerships

**Initial Investment:** €45,000–€85,000
**Startup Timeline:** 3–4 months to first production
**Breakeven:** 12–18 months (conservative estimate)

---

## 1. Business Model & Revenue Strategy

### A. Core Revenue Streams

1. **Standard Flight Cases** (80% of revenue, Year 1)
   - Off-the-shelf aluminum corner cases
   - Plywood cases with foam inserts
   - ABS hard-shell options
   - Price point: €150–€800 per unit
   - Margin: 40–60%

2. **Custom/Bespoke Cases** (15% of revenue, scaling to 30%)
   - Tailored dimensions, hardware, branding
   - Medical, military, industrial applications
   - Price point: €300–€2,000+ per unit
   - Margin: 50–70%

3. **White-Label/OEM** (5% of revenue, growth potential)
   - Manufacture for European brands
   - Bulk orders, lower per-unit pricing
   - Margin: 25–35%

### B. Pricing Strategy

| Product Type              | Cost (EUR) | Selling Price (EUR) | Margin % |
|---------------------------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Small AV Case (15L)       | €60–80    | €180–250          | 45–67%   |
| Medium AV Case (30L)      | €120–150  | €350–450          | 42–58%   |
| Large Tour Case (50L+)    | €200–280  | €600–900          | 42–55%   |
| Custom Medical Case       | €150–250  | €500–1,200        | 50–70%   |

## 11. Go-to-Market Strategy

### A. Initial Customer Acquisition (Pre-Launch & Month 1–3)
1. **B2B Outreach** — Email 100+ event companies, studios, AV integrators in Germany, Netherlands
2. **Industry Forums** — Post on professional audio forums, Gearspace, r/livesound
3. **LinkedIn** — Build brand profile, connect with procurement managers
4. **Direct Website** — Design configurator for standard cases
5. **Sample Orders** — Offer discounted first orders to establish testimonials

### B. Marketing Channels (Year 1)
- **B2B Websites** — Alibaba, Global Sources (white-label leads)
- **E-commerce** — Shopify store, Google Shopping
- **SEO** — "Flight case manufacturer Bulgaria," "custom AV cases EU"
- **Partnerships** — Contact distributors, resellers in EU
- **Trade Shows** — InfoComm, Prolight+Sound (start Year 2)

### C. Pricing Strategy for Outreach
- **Early Bird Discount:** 15–20% off for first 10 customers
- **Bulk Pricing:** 10% off 20+ units, 15% off 50+ units
- **White-Label:** 30% margin for OEM customers (negotiate)
